Privacy Policy — Be You Cosmetics

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · All Idea2App policies

This policy explains how Be You Cosmetics (the app, published by Idea2App) handles information when you use the Android app com.idea2App.gift_printing.

Information we process

Account and order information

If you create an account, sign in, or place an order, the store processes details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order information. These details are used to provide the store, fulfil orders, and respond to support requests.

Push notification token

With notifications enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an app installation token to deliver order and store updates. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

Location

If you grant location access, the app uses your location when you choose a location-based store feature, such as placing a delivery pin on the checkout map. Location is requested for that feature and you can revoke permission in Android settings.

Calls

If you choose an in-app call action, Android may ask for phone permission so the app can place that call. The app does not initiate calls on its own.

Basic technical information

Basic device and app details may be processed to deliver notifications, maintain service security, and diagnose technical issues. The app does not include advertising or analytics features for cross-app tracking.

How information is used

  • To provide the Be You Cosmetics catalogue, account, and order features.
  • To arrange delivery and respond to customer support requests.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure and meet legal obligations.

Information is not sold. Payment details entered at checkout are handled by the payment provider presented in the store flow.

Retention and your choices

Account and order information is retained while needed to provide the service and afterwards as required for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes. Notification tokens are removed when no longer valid. You can manage location and notification permissions in Android settings.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, email info@idea2app.tech with “Be You Cosmetics privacy request” in the subject. Order records that must be retained by law may remain for the required period.

Children

The app is a general shopping service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Idea2App at info@idea2app.tech or visit idea2app.tech.
