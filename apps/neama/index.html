Privacy Policy — فول وفلافل نعمة (Neama)

Last updated: 10 September 2026 · All Idea2App policies

This policy explains what فول وفلافل نعمة (Neama) ("the app", published by Idea2App) collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers the iOS app com.idea2App.neama and the Android app com.idea2app.neama.

Information we collect

Account information

If you create an account or sign in, we process the details you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number and delivery address — so we can identify you, show your orders, and contact you about them.

Order and transaction information

We process the items you view, add to your cart and order, so the store can fulfil and support those orders.

Push notification token

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver notifications about your orders and store updates. Firebase assigns your installation a token that lets us send those messages. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Device and diagnostic information

We may process basic technical information — device model, operating system version and app version — to keep the app working correctly and to diagnose faults.

How we use the information

  • To provide the store, your account and your orders.
  • To send notifications you have not turned off.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To keep the app secure, reliable and free of abuse.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

Data retention

We keep account and order information for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, tax or dispute-resolution purposes. Notification tokens are discarded when they expire or when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent for notifications or device permissions at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below. To delete your account and its data, email us and we will action it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
