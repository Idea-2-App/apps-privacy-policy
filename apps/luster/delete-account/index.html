How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@idea2app.tech with the subject "Delete my account - Luster".
  2. Send it from the email address registered on your Luster account, or include the phone number registered on it, so we can find the account.
  3. We confirm the request by replying to you, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email address and phone number.
  • Your saved delivery addresses and favourites.
  • Your notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

Order and invoice records that we must keep for legal, tax or dispute-resolution purposes are retained for as long as that obligation lasts, then deleted. They are not used for any other purpose.
